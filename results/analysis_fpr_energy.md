# Section 5.3 -- FPR-aware energy accounting

With the measured per-record false-positive rate of 0.90 (fraction of 120-s seizure-free records producing at least one alarm), a day of 720 such records yields about 641 spurious Level-2 episodes in addition to the 8 assumed genuine events, i.e. about 649 episodes/day at 0.599 energy units each (389 units/day), versus 1244 units/day for a continuous open-loop push of amplitude 0.06 on 4 nodes. The crossover is 2069 false episodes/day.
